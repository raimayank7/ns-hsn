HSN code finder and checker

Find the right chapter for your goods or the right SAC heading for your services, check if a code has enough digits for your turnover, and clean up the HSN codes of the items you bill. Then confirm the exact rate on the official GST portal. Free, no login.

How to use the HSN code finder

  1. Check a code. Type an HSN or SAC code. You see its chapter or SAC heading at once, and whether it has enough digits for your turnover.
  2. Set your turnover. Choose "Up to ₹5 crore" or "Above ₹5 crore" (last financial year). Tick "Bill to an unregistered buyer" for B2C bills.
  3. Find a chapter. Not sure of the code? Type words like "furniture", "cotton" or "repair" and pick the chapter or heading that fits.
  4. Get the exact rate. Press "Copy code and open GST portal HSN search". The official search shows the full 6 or 8-digit codes and their GST rates.
  5. Fix your items. The list at the bottom checks every item and service you saved. Type the right code or GST % and press Save.

What it does for you

  • All HS chapters and SAC headings: the 21 sections and 97 chapters for goods (chapter 77 is empty) and the SAC headings 9954 to 9999 for services.
  • Digits rule built in: at least 4 digits up to ₹5 crore turnover (may be left out on B2C bills), at least 6 above ₹5 crore, as set by Notification 78/2020-Central Tax.
  • Catches wrong codes: a code of the wrong length, a chapter that does not exist, a service with a goods code or goods with a SAC.
  • Checks your GST %: flags a rate that is not one of the rates in use since 1 Feb 2026.
  • No guessed rates: GST rates change by notification, so we send you to the official search for the exact rate instead of printing an old one.
  • In your language: the screens work in 24 languages; HSN and SAC names stay in their official English.

Your data stays with you

Codes are checked inside your browser and nothing is sent to us. Your items are kept in this browser and shared with the other Nivesh Sahayak tools through my.niveshsahayak.in, so a code you fix here is right on your next bill.

Keep a backup. If the browser's data is cleared, saved work in it is gone. Open My data (top of the page) and press Save a backup file now and then.

Questions people ask

Is this HSN code finder free?

Yes. There is no login and no limit. It works in your browser.

Why does it not show the GST rate for my code?

The rate depends on the full code and the description in the official notifications, which changed in September 2025 and again in 2026. We send you to the official GST portal search, which always shows the current rate.

How many HSN digits do I need on my invoice?

At least 4 digits if your turnover last year was up to ₹5 crore (they may be left out on bills to unregistered buyers), and at least 6 digits on every invoice above ₹5 crore, under Notification 78/2020-Central Tax.

What is the difference between HSN and SAC?

HSN codes classify goods; SAC codes classify services and always start with 99.

Can I fix the HSN of my saved items here?

Yes. The list at the bottom shows every saved item with its code and GST %. Type the right values and press Save; the next bill uses them.
